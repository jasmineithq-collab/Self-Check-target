# Sample returns and refunds policy

- Submit a return request within **14 calendar days of recorded delivery**. Include the invoice number and reason.
- For damaged or incorrect items, include photos and retain the packaging until the claim is reviewed.
- Perishable items can be returned only for a verified quality, damage, or fulfilment issue.
- Approved refunds are normally issued to the original payment method within **5–10 business days after approval**. Refer to the refund tracker for the recorded status.
- Refer to the authorized returns team for eligibility decisions.
